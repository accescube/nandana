About Nandana

Compassionate & Respectful Psychological Support

Nandana Sreekumar is a Consultant Psychologist with a Master’s degree in Psychology from the School of Behavioural Sciences, Mahatma Gandhi University.

With training and clinical exposure across mental health, child development, educational, and organizational settings, she is committed to providing compassionate and respectful psychological support.

Her approach is centred on empathy, active listening, confidentiality, professional ethics, and building a trusting therapeutic relationship. She believes that every individual has a unique story and deserves a space where they can express themselves without fear of judgment.
