# Nandana Sreekumar | Consultant Psychologist Portfolio

A minimalist, high-end personal portfolio website for **Nandana Sreekumar**, Consultant Psychologist (M.Sc. Psychology, School of Behavioural Sciences, Mahatma Gandhi University).

> *"A safe space to be heard, understood, and supported. Your thoughts, emotions, and experiences matter."*

---

## 🌟 Core Highlights

- **Aesthetic**: Luxury blue and white harmony with soothing glassmorphic elements and high-contrast typography (`Playfair Display` + `Plus Jakarta Sans`).
- **About Nandana**: Training and clinical exposure across mental health, child development, educational, and organizational settings, grounded in empathy, active listening, confidentiality, professional ethics, and trusting therapeutic rapport.
- **5 Areas of Professional Interest**:
  1. *Counselling & Emotional Wellbeing*: Support for individuals navigating emotional and personal challenges.
  2. *Psychological Assessment*: Training in assessment and interpretation across personality, intelligence, neuropsychological, and projective domains.
  3. *Child & Adolescent Support*: Academic and practical exposure to child development and educational settings.
  4. *Health & Mental Health*: Specialized training in Health Psychology, Psychological First Aid, and counselling and psychotherapy.
  5. *Research & Psychology Education*: A strong interest in psychological research, teaching, environmental psychology, and organizational psychology.
- **Her Philosophy**:
  > *“You don't have to have everything figured out before asking for support.”*  
  > Psychological support is not about having all the answers. It is about having a safe space to explore your thoughts, understand your experiences, and discover healthier ways forward.
- **Begin Your Journey**: Interactive consultation booking desk with direct WhatsApp integration, form validation, and national mental health crisis helpline notice.

## 💻 Getting Started

### Local Setup
1. Clone the repository:
   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/accescube/nandana.git
   ```
2. Open `index.html` in any modern web browser or serve via Apache / XAMPP:
   ```bash
   # Place in xampp/htdocs/NANDANA and visit:
   http://localhost/NANDANA/
   ```
